PROVENANCE AND TIMING

capture-inventory.json records each original URL, exact Wayback replay,
archive timestamp, retrieval metadata and exhibit hashes. The historical
archive timestamp must not be confused with the 28 October 2026 retrieval.

For the September 2023 house-edge article, only the capture date is known.
Its precise capture time was not logged. The separately labelled saved_at
is the TXT file's modification time, and catalogued_at is a later metadata
entry time; neither is represented as the actual acquisition time. Other
capture timestamps were recorded in the browser capture session or
immediately after the capture files were saved, as specified in metadata.

bytes; it does not independently authenticate the historical source claims.
The screenshots were visually inspected and contain public article content,
with no investigation account details or unrelated chat. The texts retain
public article content and browser accessibility structure.

LIMITS

These are historical documentation checkpoints. They do not establish
uninterrupted game deployment, exact server settlement precision, or
historical aggregate losses. Preserve rounded values and original spelling
when quoting, and distinguish factual source wording from interpretation.

No failed HTTP challenge pages or raw identifying bet-dialog captures are
included. Sanitized historical bet tables are provided separately.
