What is the house edge on 500 Casino?
June 6, 2026

The house edge is a common mathematical advantage that our games have in order to remain profitable in the long run.

 

Wheel of Fortune
Every color has an individual house edge.
The weighted average house edge for the Wheel of Fortune is ~5.01%.

Gray has 26 out of the 54 spots available on the Wheel. Probability is ~48.15%. House edge is ~3.7%.

Red has 17 out of the 54 spots available on the Wheel. Probability is ~31.48%. House edge is ~5.5%

Blue has 10 out of the 54 spots available on the Wheel. Probability is ~18.51%. House edge is ~7.4%

Gold has 1 out of the 54 spots available on the Wheel. Probability is ~1.85%. House edge is ~7.4%

Roulette

Every color has an individual house edge.
​The weighted average house edge for Roulette is ~6.66%.

Black has 7 out of the 15 spots available on the reel. Probability is ~46.66%. House edge is ~6.66%

Red has 7 out of the 15 spots available on the reel. Probability is ~46.66%. House edge is ~6.66%

Green has 1 out of the 15 spots available on the reel. Probability is ~6.66%. House edge is ~6.66%

Duels

Duels don't have a "house edge". Instead, 500 Casino takes a fixed 5% rake for every played duel.

 

Crash

Crash doesn't technically have a "house edge". We pre-generated 10 million Crash rounds and we used a coefficient to statistically determine that 6% of the rounds instantly crash at 1x.

 

Plinko

Every board was manually adjusted so that the house edge over 10 million simulated rounds is roughly equal to 4%, respectively 8% for the extreme mode.

Cases and Case Battles

For Cases and Case Battles, we are using a standard formula that combines all the item chances and items values in a case, then adds a house edge of 10% on top to determine the final price of a case. 


Casino

Casino games rely on a coefficient called "RTP" (Return to Player) which is the statistical average of payouts over billions of rounds. Every game has a different RTP. They can vary from 92% to 99.65%, depending on provider and other factors. We are displaying the game's RTP in the bottom right corner of the screen if it's available when you play.
