What are the odds of Towers?
April 29, 2024

Towers offer different odds based on the difficulty level selected by a user.

 

The odds of Easy difficulty is 0.6666 per level. 

The odds of Medium difficulty is 0.50 per level.  

The odds of Hard difficulty is 0.3333 per level. 
​

Towers has a fixed house edge set at 5% for all 3 difficulty modes.
