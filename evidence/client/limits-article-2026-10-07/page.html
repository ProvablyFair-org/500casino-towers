What Are the Limits of Towers?

May 21, 2024

Towers has a global win limit of $200,000 per round. The maximum allowed bet is thus adjusted and limited based on the the difficulty mode chosen by the user. The minimum bet is set at 0.10 USD.

  • The maximum multiplier for Easy is ~34,5x.

  • The maximum multiplier for Medium is ~613x.

  • The maximum multiplier for Difficult is ~35,354x.

The maximum bet allowed is thus based on the above multipliers. For medium difficulty, the maximum bet is up to ~$326.20 per round.
